# Insight Capsule — Strand: parser — Pulse 1 (2026-06-29)

**Scope**: `src/parser.ts` (read-only on `src/types.ts`)
**Status**: completed, test-green (`test/parser.spec.ts`)

## What was built
- `parse(input: string): RawRecord[]` — splits the raw feed into one RawRecord
  per non-skipped physical line. (executed)

## Raw feed grammar (discovered + locked)
- Each record line is a list of `key=value` pairs separated by `;`. (executed)
- Keys are lowercased; values are trimmed. (executed)
- Recognized keys: `id`, `ts` (ISO-8601 UTC string), `amount` (decimal MAJOR
  units, e.g. `42.00`), `cur` (ISO-4217 code). (inspected)
- Blank lines and `#`-comment lines are skipped. (executed)
- Tokens with no `=` are ignored. (executed)

## Key decision
- The parser does NOT convert units or rename fields — it preserves raw strings.
  All conversion is deferred to the normalizer (Strand 2). (executed)

## Provenance
- Each RawRecord carries `lineNo`, the 1-based PHYSICAL line number (skipped
  lines still count), so downstream strands can trace a record to its source.
  (executed)

## Forward implications
- Strand 2 (normalizer) consumes `RawRecord.fields` by these exact key names and
  owns all unit conversion + the `id`-required rule. (assumed)
- `lineNo` flows through to the canonical `sourceLine` field. (assumed)
